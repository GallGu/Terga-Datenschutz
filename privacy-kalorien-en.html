Privacy Policy

App “Calories” (Kaloriensaldo) · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This is a translation. The German version is legally binding; in case of any discrepancy, the German version prevails.

Summary: Calories stores your nutrition data locally on your device and does not require an account. Health data from Apple Health is processed only with your explicit permission and exclusively on your devices (iPhone and, if applicable, Apple Watch). When you search by barcode or name, only the barcode or search term is sent to the food database Open Food Facts – without any link to your person. We do not track you, do not show ads and do not share any data with third parties.

1. Controller

Controller for data processing within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Günter Gall
  • Contact: entwickler.gall@web.de

2. Data stored locally

The entries you record (meals, foods, nutritional values, targets, weight and settings) are stored exclusively on your device. There are no user accounts, no sign-in and no storage of your nutrition data on servers operated by us or third parties. There is no tracking and no analysis of your usage; no analytics, advertising or other third-party services (SDKs) are used.

3. Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit)

Calories can access Apple Health – only with your explicit permission. You decide in the Health permission prompt which access you allow, and you can revoke it at any time in the Health app.

Reading (optional)

  • Active and resting energy – to include them in your daily budget if you wish.
  • Body weight (most recent value) – as the basis for the macro target.

Writing (optional)

  • Calories and macronutrients eaten (protein, carbohydrates, fat) – so that your entries appear in Apple Health.

Apple Watch (optional)

If you install the Calories app on your Apple Watch and allow access there, it reads from Apple Health on the watch:

  • Today’s active and resting energy – for your daily budget.
  • Today’s steps.

The watch shows these values in its complications on the watch face. The iPhone app sends today’s calories eaten and your budget settings (manual active and resting energy and whether the budget is taken from Apple Health) directly to your paired Apple Watch so that the watch shows the same values as the iPhone. The watch stores the most recently shown values locally so that it can keep showing them while the watch is locked.

This health data is exchanged exclusively on your devices between Calories and Apple Health. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties, not used for advertising and not used for data mining or similar purposes. If you do not grant permission, the app continues to work without these values.

4. Camera (barcode scanning)

When you use the barcode scanner, the app accesses the camera – after your permission – to recognise the barcode of a food. The camera is used solely for real-time barcode recognition. No photos or videos are stored or transmitted.

5. Nutrition lookup via Open Food Facts

To load nutritional values for a scanned barcode or a search term you enter, the app sends a request to the open, non-profit food database Open Food Facts. Only the respective barcode or search term is transmitted. No personal data, no identifiers and none of your health or nutrition data are transmitted; the request is made without an account.

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Nutritional values already retrieved are cached locally on your device for faster display. Data processing by Open Food Facts is subject to their own privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

As with any internet connection, the IP address and standard device information are technically transmitted to the service. We ourselves do not receive this information.

6. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. No data is shared with third parties – except for the nutrition lookup at Open Food Facts that you trigger yourself, as described in section 5.

7. Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data about you or store it on our own servers, we hold no such data that we could disclose, correct or delete. You can remove your locally stored data yourself at any time in the app or by deleting the app (on the iPhone and, if applicable, the Apple Watch); you manage and delete values written to Apple Health in the Health app.

Irrespective of this, the GDPR generally grants you the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

8. Children

Calories is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this privacy policy

We may adapt this privacy policy if the app or the legal framework changes. The current version is available at the address given in your App Store listing. The date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about data protection, you can reach us at entwickler.gall@web.de.
