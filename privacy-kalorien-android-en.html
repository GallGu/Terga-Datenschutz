Privacy Policy

App “Calories” for Android and Wear OS · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This is a translation. The German version is legally binding; in case of any discrepancy, the German version prevails.

Summary: Calories stores your nutrition data locally on your device and does not require an account. Health data from Health Connect and – on the watch – from the health services of Wear OS is processed only with your explicit permission and on your devices. When you search by barcode or name, only the barcode or search term is sent to the food database Open Food Facts – without any link to your person. For barcode scanning, backup and the connection to the watch, the app uses services of the operating system or of Google Play services. We do not track you, do not show ads and do not share any data with third parties.

1. Controller

Controller for data processing within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Günter Gall
  • Contact: entwickler.gall@web.de

2. Data stored locally

The entries you record (meals, foods, nutritional values, targets, weight and settings) are stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts, no sign-in and no storage of your nutrition data on servers operated by us. There is no tracking and no analysis of your usage; no analytics or advertising services (SDKs) are used.

3. Backup (Android backup)

Your entries and settings are included in Android’s backup. If you have turned on backup on your device, Android stores this data in your Google Account and restores it on a new device; it is also carried over when you transfer to a new device. The backup is managed by Android, not by the app: you turn it on or off in the system settings, and Google’s terms and privacy policy apply. Cached nutritional values from Open Food Facts are excluded from the backup.

4. Health data (Health Connect)

Calories can access Health Connect, Android’s health data store on your device – only with your explicit permission. You decide in the permission prompt which access you allow, and you can revoke it at any time in the Health Connect settings.

Reading (optional)

  • Active calories burned and basal metabolic rate – to include them in your daily budget if you wish.
  • Body weight (most recent value) – as the basis for the macro target.

Writing (optional)

  • Calories and macronutrients eaten (protein, carbohydrates, fat) together with the name of the food – so that your entries appear in Health Connect.

This health data is exchanged exclusively on your device between Calories and Health Connect. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties, not used for advertising and not used for data mining or similar purposes. If you do not grant permission, the app continues to work without these values.

Watch with Wear OS (optional)

If you install Calories on your watch and allow access to your physical activity there, the watch receives from the health services of Wear OS:

  • today’s steps,
  • today’s calories burned – for your daily budget.

The watch shows these values in its complications on the watch face and stores the most recently received values locally. The phone app sends today’s calories eaten and your budget settings (manual active and resting energy and whether the budget is taken from Health Connect) to your paired watch so that the watch shows the same values as the phone. The transfer uses the data connection of Google Play services between your paired devices; if phone and watch are not directly connected, it may pass through Google’s servers. No other health data leaves the watch.

5. Barcode scanning

The barcode scanner is provided by Google Play services (“Scanned by Google on behalf of Kalorien”). The app itself has no access to the camera: recognition takes place on your device, and the app only receives the recognised digits of the barcode. No photos or videos are stored or transmitted to us. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to Google Play services.

6. Nutrition lookup via Open Food Facts

To load nutritional values for a scanned barcode or a search term you enter, the app sends a request to the open, non-profit food database Open Food Facts. Only the respective barcode or search term is transmitted. No personal data, no identifiers and none of your health or nutrition data are transmitted; the request is made without an account.

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Nutritional values already retrieved are cached locally on your device for faster display. Data processing by Open Food Facts is subject to their own privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

As with any internet connection, the IP address and standard device information are technically transmitted to the service. We ourselves do not receive this information.

7. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. No data is shared with third parties – except for the nutrition lookup at Open Food Facts that you trigger yourself, as described in section 6, and the services of Android and Google Play services described in sections 3 to 5, which you turn on or trigger yourself.

8. Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data about you or store it on our own servers, we hold no such data that we could disclose, correct or delete. You can remove your locally stored data yourself at any time in the app or by deleting the app (on the phone and, if applicable, the watch); you manage and delete values written to Health Connect in the Health Connect settings, and your backup in the settings of Android or your Google Account.

Irrespective of this, the GDPR generally grants you the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

9. Children

Calories is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may adapt this privacy policy if the app or the legal framework changes. The current version is available at the address given in your store listing. The date above identifies the current version.

11. Contact
